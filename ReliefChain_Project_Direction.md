# ReliefChain - Project Direction, Features, and Roles

## 1. Project Direction
**Elevator Pitch:** ReliefChain is a purpose-built disaster relief distribution platform that enables government agencies and humanitarian organizations to distribute emergency financial assistance quickly, transparently, and accountably using the Stellar blockchain.

**The Problem:** The Philippines is highly disaster-prone. Current disaster response distribution often involves manual beneficiary verification, delayed disbursement, long queues, limited fund visibility, and auditing difficulties across multiple organizations. Existing digital wallets aren't tailored for disaster relief management.

**The Solution:** An end-to-end platform designed specifically for managing and distributing emergency financial assistance. Rather than replacing existing payment systems, it leverages Stellar as the financial infrastructure for fast settlement, low fees, and an auditable, tamper-resistant ledger.

## 2. Features
- **Aid Program Management:** Create and manage aid programs by defining the disaster event, budget allocation, assistance amount per beneficiary, and target number of beneficiaries.
- **Beneficiary Verification:** Register citizens or import existing records. Allows authorized personnel to verify applicants to reduce duplicate claims and ensure eligibility.
- **Financial Aid Distribution:** Near-instant, direct transfer of emergency assistance to the beneficiary's Stellar wallet, eliminating manual cash handling.
- **Purpose-Based Digital Assistance:** Distribute aid as digital vouchers (e.g., Food, Medicine, School Supplies) that can only be redeemed at accredited merchants for their intended purpose.
- **Merchant Redemption:** Accredited merchants accept digital aid via QR-based payments. Transactions are automatically recorded on Stellar, and the beneficiary's remaining balance is updated.
- **Transparency and Monitoring Dashboard:** Real-time tracking of total allocated/distributed funds, remaining balance, verified beneficiaries, distribution progress, and complete transaction history.
- **Stellar Integration (Wallet & Cash-In/Out):** Connects to existing Stellar-compatible wallets (eliminating custom wallet development) and provides On/Off ramps for converting digital assets to Philippine Pesos.

## 3. Roles
- **Government Agencies & Humanitarian Organizations:** Create disaster relief programs, define budgets and targets, and monitor the distribution process and overall program progress.
- **Authorized Personnel:** Review, verify, and approve beneficiary registrations to ensure only eligible individuals receive aid.
- **Beneficiaries (Citizens):** Register for aid, receive digital financial assistance or purpose-based vouchers in their connected Stellar wallets, and redeem them at participating merchants.
- **Accredited Merchants:** Accept digital vouchers or financial assistance via QR payments for specific goods (like groceries or pharmacies) and receive immediate settlement through the Stellar network.
- **Authorized Donors / Auditors:** Access the transparency dashboard to monitor fund allocation, completed transactions, and ensure accountability through an auditable ledger.
